✦ Goal Lab

A goal is a result you want to bring about. It describes what will be different when you succeed. An action is something you do to move toward that result.

For example, “find a career direction that fits me” is a goal. “Research three occupations” is an action toward it.

Not sure exactly what you want yet? That's okay. Work through these four short steps at your own pace.
